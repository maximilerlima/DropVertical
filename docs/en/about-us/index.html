Frequently asked questions

Can I book in English or Spanish?

The website and booking form are available in Portuguese, English and Spanish. Let the team know your preferred language on WhatsApp.

Is equipment included? How long is a lesson?

Ask the team to confirm equipment, duration, prices and cancellation terms for your chosen activity before booking.

Do I need previous experience?

Surf lessons cater to beginners, intermediate and advanced surfers. For other activities and rentals, tell the team about your experience so they can discuss the right format.

What should I bring?

Before you book, the team will explain what to bring and confirm the details of your experience at Posto 6.

Drop Vertical

A school
with history

Drop Vertical was founded by Daniel Souto, a Barra da Tijuca surf reference who has shaped new generations of surfers for decades.

More than a school, we are a community that lives the ocean every day. We teach in a practical, easy, safe and fun way, so you can progress at your own pace and discover everything the ocean has to offer.

“Teaching surfing has always been about sharing freedom, respect and joy. The ocean has the power to transform lives.”Daniel Souto
Founder of Drop Vertical
